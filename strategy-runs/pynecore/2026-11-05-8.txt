Stage 1. PyneCore run of 15M Window Order Block Strategy on the fitted sample,
1 May 2026 through 30 October 2026. One MES contract, $5 per point, no commission.
Each outright is replayed only while it is the daily volume leader.
MESM6 is 1 May through 12 June. MESU6 is 14 June through 13 September.
MESZ6 is 14 September through 30 October. Times are New York.

Parameters
HTF OB: 15
LTF OB: empty, so the 1-minute chart
Window: 9:30 until 16:00 New York. 16:00 is not included.
Minute window: 0 to 60
Skip Sundays: off
Liquidity Timeframe: 4H
HTF Confirm: 45 to 5, both ends included. This wraps the hour.
LTF Confirm: 0 to 15, both ends included.
Qualify by: Close
Timeframe Target: 15M
Target Swing Length: 2
Max Target: 2
Order From: 10:05 New York
Max Stop: 15 points
One order per New York day.
Flat At 15:55: on

The higher-timeframe block is the last block of the hour. Its confirming
candle opens from :45 through :05. The lower-timeframe block has to overlap
that block and confirm in the following hour's opening, from :00 through :15.
The retest that prints the order has to fall in that same wick. The 4-hour
sweep, premium and discount, and the session rules are unchanged. The
indicator file is unchanged.
