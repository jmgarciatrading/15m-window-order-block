The higher-timeframe block is the last block of the hour. Its confirming
candle opens from :45 through :05. The lower-timeframe block has to overlap
that block and confirm in the following hour's opening, from :00 through :15.
The retest that prints the order has to fall in that same wick. The 4-hour
sweep is off. A stop or target that trades fills on the one-minute path.
The open walks to the nearer extreme first, and an equal distance walks to
the high first. An open trade is marked to that path. The indicator file
is unchanged.
