The model is lab/window_order_block.py.

Parameters

LTF OB: the 1-minute bar
Window: 9:30 until 16:00 New York. 16:00 is not included.
Minute window: 0 to 60
Skip Sundays: off
Liquidity Timeframe: 4H
HTF Confirm: 45 to 5, both ends included. This wraps the hour.
LTF Confirm: 0 to 15, both ends included.
Qualify by: Close
Timeframe Target: 15M
Target Swing Length: 2
Max Target: 2
Order From: 10:05 New York
Max Stop: 15 points
One order per New York day.
Flat At 15:55: on

The higher-timeframe block is the last block of the hour. Its confirming
candle opens from :45 through :05. The lower-timeframe block has to overlap
that block and confirm in the following hour's opening, from :00 through :15.
The retest that prints the order has to fall in that same wick. A stop or
target that trades fills on the one-minute path. The open walks to the nearer
extreme first, and an equal distance walks to the high first. An open trade
is marked to that path. The indicator file is unchanged.
